Tailored to you

Our set programmes suit most families. If yours needs a different shape, choose the length, the hours, the meals and the treatments here, and watch the fee update as you go.

The fee is a guide, not a quote or a booking. We confirm your programme and fee after a consultation, once we know about your birth, your home and how you’re recovering.

What matters most to you?

As you continue to build your bespoke programme, recommendations will be highlighted.

Live-in or live-out?

The care is the same either way. The difference is whether your specialist is in your home overnight.

Daytime or day and night care

With night care, your specialist cares for your baby through the night while you rest. It covers 12 hours, arranged around your routine, and your baby is back with you by morning.

Night care is available on live-in programmes. Go back to step 01 and choose live-in care to add it.

Recovery treatments

Herbal rinse and belly binding are part of every programme. Choose the treatments you’d like alongside them.

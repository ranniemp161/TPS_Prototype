Build the care you have in mind

Our four programmes suit most families. If yours needs a different shape, choose the length, the hours, the meals and the treatments here, and watch an indicative fee update as you go.

It isn’t a quote or a booking. We confirm your programme and fee after a consultation, once we know about your birth, your home and how you’re recovering.

Live-in, or daytime?

The care is the same either way. The difference is whether your specialist is in your home overnight.

She stays in your home for the whole programme.

She arrives each morning and leaves in the evening. Adds £30 a day towards her travel and accommodation.

How long?

Thirty days is the full confinement month. Changing the length resets the massage rhythm to that programme’s standard, so you always start from a complete programme.

Daytime, or day and night?

With night care you sleep through while your specialist has your baby. She starts at 1pm, settles at 7pm, takes your baby from 11.30pm and brings her back to you at 6am.

About The Postpartum Suite

What is The Postpartum Suite?

The Postpartum Suite provides bespoke, in-home postpartum care inspired by Malaysian traditions. Your care includes postpartum treatments, freshly prepared meals, protected time to rest and recover, newborn support, laundry and light household support.

What makes The Postpartum Suite different?

We offer a level of expertise and care that sets us apart. Our specialists are trained in traditional postpartum care, take great pride in what they do, and each has extensive experience in the field. Rather than offering treatments as a one off, we make recovery part of every day, with one dedicated specialist caring for you and your baby in your own home.

Where do you provide care?

We provide care across England.

Are you insured?

Yes, we are fully insured.

Do you have reviews?

Yes. We are proud of the feedback we receive from the families we care for, and we are happy to share reviews with you.

Is my privacy protected?

Absolutely. Confidentiality and discretion are fundamental to the way we care for every family. The personal details you share with us during your consultation and throughout your care are held securely and treated in the strictest confidence. We never sell or share your information with third parties, and it is only ever disclosed where we are required to do so by law. Our specialists understand the trust that comes with being welcomed into your home, and they care for you and your family with complete discretion at all times. Full details are in our privacy policy.

Programmes and Personalisation

What programmes do you offer?

We offer 5, 7, 14 and 30 day programmes. Every programme includes the same standard of care. The difference is how long your support is in place.

What is included in a programme?

Every programme is centred on your recovery. It includes postpartum treatments, three freshly cooked meals a day, postpartum drinks and teas, protected time to rest, newborn support, laundry and ironing, and light household support.

How is my care personalised?

Before your programme begins, we take time to understand your recovery, feeding preferences, household routine, dietary requirements and the areas where you would most value support. Your specialist then shapes each day around these priorities.

Can the day be adapted around me and my baby?

Yes. Each day is arranged around your recovery, your baby's routine and the times when you would benefit most from support. Reasonable changes to agreed start and finish times can also be discussed.

Can I extend my programme after it has started?

Yes, subject to availability. This includes moving from a 14 day programme to 30 days or longer. We recommend letting us know as early as possible. We always aim to keep the same specialist with you, but we cannot guarantee her availability for an extended period.

Can I book a single treatment instead of a programme?

Yes. Alongside our programmes, we offer standalone in-home treatments, including postpartum massage, so you can enjoy restorative care in the comfort of your own home without booking a full programme. Please contact us to arrange a treatment at a time that suits you.

Do you care for twins?

Yes. Caring for twins needs additional support, so each stay is tailored to your family and priced on a bespoke basis. We will talk through the details with you during your consultation.

Can I give a programme as a gift?

Yes. Gift vouchers are available for our programmes and treatments, and they make a thoughtful gift for an expectant mother.

Booking and Payment

When should I book?

We recommend booking as early as possible, ideally in your second trimester, particularly if you would like live in care. If your baby has already arrived, please get in touch and we will do our very best to help. We care for mothers and babies from birth up to four months.

What if you are fully booked?

Our programmes do become fully booked. If your dates are unavailable, you are welcome to join our waiting list, and a member of our concierge team will contact you as soon as availability opens.

How do I secure my booking?

A 10% reservation deposit secures your dates. The balance can be paid in instalments, with the final payment due four weeks before your due date, as set out in our Client Service Agreement.

What happens if my baby arrives early or late?

Your specialist is on standby from two weeks before to two weeks after your estimated due date. Please keep us updated as your due date approaches so we can coordinate the start of your care smoothly.

What is your cancellation policy?

Once your booking is confirmed, we hold your specialist for your dates and turn away other families. For this reason, all payments, including the deposit, are non-refundable after confirmation. Full details are in our terms.

Our Specialists

Who are your specialists?

Our specialists are experienced in postpartum care, postpartum treatments, traditional confinement care, feeding support and newborn care. Each has extensive experience and takes great pride in her work. Every specialist is carefully selected for her experience, qualifications and suitability, and all work to the standards of care and service set by The Postpartum Suite.

What checks and training do your specialists have?

Every specialist is enhanced DBS checked, has her references verified and is fully insured. All hold infant first aid and safeguarding training, alongside the care qualifications relevant to their role.

What is the difference between live in and live out care?

With live in care, your specialist stays in your home for the length of your programme. With live out care, she stays nearby and arrives each morning at 7am. Live out care depends on your location, and additional accommodation or travel costs may apply. We recommend live in care, as your specialist becomes a natural part of your household and its rhythm.

What do I need to provide for live in care?

A private room should be available for your specialist.

What are the hours of care?

Your specialist cares for you and your baby from 7am to 7pm, including a break during the day that is timed around you and your baby. Care continues at weekends according to your agreed schedule.

How does 24 hour care work?

Where 24 hour care has been arranged, support follows an agreed rota that gives your specialist appropriate rest while making sure care is available throughout. We agree the arrangement with you before your programme begins.

Can I share preferences about my specialist, or speak to her beforehand?

Yes. You are welcome to share any preferences during your consultation. Where possible, we can also arrange an introductory call before your care begins.

What happens if my specialist becomes unwell?

We arrange another fully trained specialist to continue your care, and we contact you promptly to confirm the arrangements.

Treatments

Which treatments are included?

Your programme includes postpartum massage and hot stone therapy several times a week, daily belly binding applied after your massage or hot stone treatment, daily herbal baths and thermal blanket treatments. On other days, your care also includes foot massage and reflexology and a full body scrub.

I had a caesarean. Can I still have treatments?

Yes, with care. Treatments and belly binding are adapted to your recovery and begin in line with the guidance of your midwife or doctor.

Mother and Baby Care

How will I be supported?

Your specialist makes sure you have protected time to rest, sleep and recover. She takes care of your baby while you rest, prepares your meals and drinks, and is on hand throughout the day so you never feel you are managing alone.

How will my baby be cared for?

Your specialist takes care of nappy changes, winding, settling and feeding, and supports you with bathing your baby. She also shares what she notices about feeding and sleep, so your confidence grows each day.

Do you support breastfeeding and bottle feeding?

Yes. If you are breastfeeding, your specialist brings your baby to you for each feed and settles them again afterwards. If you are bottle feeding, she prepares bottles and feeds your baby so you can rest.

Will my specialist come with me when I go out?

Yes. Your specialist can come with you to the doctor, to the shops or on a gentle walk. She prepares the changing bag, manages the pram and car seat, and cares for your baby while you are seen.

Is The Postpartum Suite a replacement for my midwife or health visitor?

No. Our care works alongside your NHS or private medical care, not in place of it. If anything concerns us, we will always encourage you to speak with your midwife, health visitor or GP.

Food and Drinks

What type of food do you prepare?

We cook Malaysian, Chinese, Indian and Western dishes, alongside traditional postpartum meals such as nourishing soups, broths and warming, protein rich dishes. Food is a central part of recovery, so we always recommend what is best for your health. We are also happy to follow your own recipes.

Can meals be tailored to my dietary needs and family?

Yes. Menus are adapted around your dietary requirements, allergies and preferences. Meals are prepared for your family too, with your postpartum needs always at the centre of the menu.

What drinks are included?

Your specialist prepares drinks to support your recovery, including red date tea, ginger infusions, herbal teas, lactation drinks and fresh smoothies.

Are snacks included?

Yes, on request. Your specialist will happily prepare simple, healthy snacks for you whenever you ask.

Who pays for groceries?

You cover the cost of groceries. Your specialist collects everything needed and prepares every meal fresh in your kitchen.

Household Support

What household support is included?

Your specialist takes care of the laundry for you and your baby, irons it and puts it away. She also makes your bed each day, keeps the kitchen spotless after every meal, and hoovers and mops the areas you use most. Baby items are washed and sterilised, your baby's clothes are kept organised, and your groceries are collected.

Can the specialist clean other rooms in my house?

Our care is devoted to you and your baby, so household support focuses on the areas that support your recovery. Deep cleaning is not included, as it would take time away from the care you came to us for.

Can my family be at home during the programme?

Of course. Your specialist works respectfully around partners, children and other family members, while keeping your recovery and your baby's care at the heart of every day.

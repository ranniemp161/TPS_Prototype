What is Postpartum Care?

Our postpartum care comes from the Malaysian and Southeast Asian tradition of confinement, where the first month after birth is a sacred window dedicated entirely to maternal rest, healing, and bonding.

Rather than expecting a mother to navigate the postpartum transition alone, this care surrounds her with skilled daily support, nourishing food, and restorative bodywork.

At The Postpartum Suite, we bring this time-honoured practice into your home through dedicated live-in care from morning to evening.

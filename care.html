The Care

Every programme is built from the same four kinds of care. Your specialist provides all four, every day of your stay, and the length of your programme changes how often each one repeats. This page sets out what each area actually involves.

Mother and Baby Care

Your specialist provides hands on support for your baby throughout the day, including feeding support, settling, bathing, nappy care, and accompanying you and your baby on outings. Equal attention is paid to your own recovery. This is not childcare on its own. Your specialist is caring for you as the mother, with your baby's needs met alongside it.

Recovery Treatments

Hands on treatments are built into every programme rather than offered as an occasional visit. These include postpartum massage with hot stone therapy, foot reflexology, facial massage, herbal foot treatment, and a traditional body scrub prepared fresh in your home. Daily belly binding and herbal baths are also part of your stay. How often each treatment repeats depends on the length of programme you choose, and is set out in full on the Programmes page.

Meals and Nourishment

Three meals are cooked fresh in your own kitchen each day, for you and your family, alongside herbal teas and lactation drinks. Meals are prepared with warming, iron rich ingredients in keeping with confinement tradition, and adapted to suit your preferences.

Note for Amidat

The dishes actually cooked, and how allergies and dietary preferences are accommodated, need to be confirmed here before this section is finished. A sample week of menus can be added once agreed.

Home Support

Everyday household demands are handled from the moment your specialist arrives, including laundry, ironing, light housekeeping and grocery collection. Support is centred on you and your recovery, rather than the running of the wider household.

Note for Amidat

The exact boundary of what light housekeeping covers, and which rooms your specialist works in, should be confirmed so it can be stated plainly here rather than left general.

Choosing Your Length of Care

Every programme includes all four kinds of care described above, from five days to thirty. What changes with a longer programme is how often each treatment repeats, and how far into your recovery your specialist stays with you.

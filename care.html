The weeks that follow birth are some of the most important of a mother's life. Your body is healing from pregnancy and birth, your energy is being rebuilt, and the care you receive now can shape how you feel for months to come. Yet this is often the time when a mother's own recovery comes last. At The Postpartum Suite, we put it first. From your very first days at home with your baby, we care for you in four ways: support with you and your baby, restorative postpartum treatments, fresh nourishing meals and help around your home. Together, they give you what every new mother deserves: the time and space to truly heal.

Mother and Baby Care

Hands on support with your baby, and thoughtful care for you, every day.

The weeks after birth are not meant to be managed alone. Your dedicated postpartum specialist stays with you, taking time to learn the routine of your home, your baby's cues and the way you like things done. Her care is there in the quiet early hours as much as in the busy ones, and it is guided by two priorities: your baby's comfort and your rest.

Treatments

Hands on care that repeats through your stay, not offered once as a treat.

Elsewhere, postpartum treatments are often booked as a single indulgence. Inspired by Malaysian confinement traditions, ours are woven into your stay, so warmth, support and restoration build gently day after day. Every treatment takes place in the comfort of your home, at a time that suits you and your baby.

Read the detail

Meals

Three fresh meals a day, cooked in your own kitchen, for you and your family.

In postpartum care, also known as confinement, food is an essential part of healing. Rather than meals arriving in delivery boxes, your specialist shops and cooks in your kitchen each day, so every dish is served hot, fresh and suited to that stage of your recovery. The menu is guided by Malaysian confinement principles, using warming ingredients such as ginger, turmeric and black pepper, slow simmered broths, and gentle dishes that are easy to digest. Cold and raw foods are avoided in the early weeks. Your family dines from the same table, and dietary requirements, allergies and preferences are discussed with you before we arrive.

Home Support

Everyday life, taken care of from the moment we arrive.

A new baby brings more laundry, more dishes and more errands almost overnight. Your specialist attends to these quietly and discreetly, so your home remains calm and ordered and your only focus is rest. This is not a visiting service that comes and goes. It is care woven into the whole day.

Mother and Baby Care

Care for your baby while you rest

Whether you need an uninterrupted sleep after a long night or a moment to bathe in peace, your baby is in attentive, experienced hands. Your specialist takes care of changing, winding, settling, bathing support, and feeding with expressed or formula milk. If you are breastfeeding, she brings your baby to you for each feed and settles them again afterwards, so your energy is kept for the moments that matter most. On selected programmes this care continues through the night. Along the way she gently shares what she observes, from feeding cues to sleep patterns, so your confidence grows with each day.

Accompanying you and baby on outings

Those first trips out of the house should feel effortless. Your specialist can accompany you to GP appointments, baby clinic weigh ins or a gentle stroll in the park. She prepares the changing bag, manages the car seat and pram, and holds your baby while you are seen, so you step out feeling composed and cared for.

Treatments

Postpartum massage and hot stone therapy, several times a week

At the heart of your recovery is postpartum massage, carried out several times each week. Slow, warming strokes ease the tension of pregnancy, birth and early feeding, while heated stones bring a deep, soothing warmth to the body. Each session is tailored to how you are feeling, leaving you relaxed, restored and wonderfully cared for.

Belly binding, daily

Your tummy wrap is applied once your massage or hot stone therapy is complete, while your body is warm and relaxed, so it supports and holds the benefits of your treatment. This gives your core and lower back a reassuring, held feeling as your body recovers, and extra support as you lift, carry and feed your baby. Each wrap is adjusted to your comfort. After a caesarean birth, we follow the guidance of your midwife or doctor on when to begin.

Herbal baths for you, daily

Each day your specialist prepares a warm bath infused with fresh herbs and leaves, such as lemongrass and pandan. It is a calming feeling that soothes tired muscles, refreshes the skin and offers a quiet moment that is entirely yours.

Thermal blanket treatment

You rest comfortably wrapped in a heated blanket as a deep, steady warmth builds, leaving you free to simply relax. Each session is tailored to how you are feeling that day.

Foot massage, reflexology and full body scrub

On the days between your massages, your care continues with a soothing foot massage and reflexology or a gentle full body scrub, so there is always something restorative in your day.

Meals

Fresh postpartum meals, 3 a day

Breakfast, lunch and dinner are prepared from scratch each day. Dishes might include ginger fish soup, chicken braised in ginger and herbs, comforting rice porridge, slow cooked broths and greens stir fried with garlic. The menu evolves throughout your stay as your body recovers.

Herbal teas

Your specialist brews tea fresh throughout the day as required. Warm drinks are favoured over cold, so there is always something warm within reach.

Lactation support drinks

Warm drinks made with ingredients traditionally given to nursing mothers, such as oats, dates and fenugreek, are prepared fresh each day. If you are not breastfeeding, we prepare other nourishing warm drinks in their place.

Home Support

Laundry and ironing

Your laundry is taken care of throughout your stay. Your clothes and your baby's are washed, beautifully pressed and put away, and your bed is made fresh each day so it is always inviting when you return to rest.

Light house cleaning, organising baby wardrobe

The kitchen is left spotless after every meal, and the areas of your home you use most are hoovered and mopped, so everything stays fresh and orderly. Your baby's wardrobe is kept neatly organised, so every outfit, muslin and essential is exactly where you need it.

Grocery collection

Your specialist collects your groceries along with the fresh produce and specialist ingredients for your meals, so the kitchen is always well stocked and nobody needs to make a last minute trip to the shops.

While you rest the rooms you live in are looked after

  • The living room is where your days with your baby unfold, so we keep it feeling restful and composed. Throughout the day the space is quietly tidied, and the floors are hoovered and mopped so the room always feels fresh. Whenever you come down to sit with your baby or welcome family, it is serene, orderly and ready for you, as though it has been waiting.

  • Three fresh meals are cooked in your kitchen every day, made for you, your family and your recovery. Your groceries are collected, warm teas and lactation drinks are prepared throughout the day, and your baby's bottles and feeding items are washed and sterilised. After l meals, the kitchen is restored, with surfaces cleaned and floors hoovered and mopped, so it is never something you have to think about.

  • Your clothes and your baby's are washed, beautifully pressed and put away, with your baby's wardrobe sorted and neatly organised. Every essential, from nappies to muslins, is kept stocked and within easy reach. Whether it is a 3am feed or an afternoon change, you step into a room that is peaceful, considered and already prepared, with nothing to search for.

One person

Your Postpartum Specialist

One dedicated person in your home, chosen with care.

The person who looks after you is the heart of your stay. Every specialist at The Postpartum Suite has extensive experience in caring for mothers and newborns and is trained in our approach to recovery, from treatments and binding to the preparation of postpartum meals. Before anyone joins us, they go through an enhanced DBS check, reference checks and an interview process. All of our specialists are fully insured and hold infant first aid and safeguarding training.

Your specialist takes time to understand your home, your routine and the kind of support you want. You will know who is arriving before your stay begins, and you will have the same specialist throughout, so the care feels personal from the first day to the last.

Your care begins well before your postpartum specialist arrives.

We start with a consultation to understand your birth plan, your home and family, and what you most want from your recovery. Your pre-programme form then lets you share your dietary needs, preferences and the details that make your home yours.

From there, your stay is shaped around you: when your treatments take place, what is cooked, how your baby's day runs and how much quiet time you would like.

If your birth or recovery plan changes, your care adapts with you, following the guidance of your midwife or doctor.

You can stay with us for 5, 7, 14 or 30 days, with care from 7am to 7pm each day and night support available if required.

## 3. Worst inconsistencies (plain language)

Caveat: static read at load, no scrolling. Pinned and scrubbed sections (what-we-do, index) may be mid transform, so a few very large values (what-we-do 109 to 115px "Who is it for?" and "Tailored To You") are display lines measured at their rest state, not a bug in the read.

1. Page titles run from 32px to 160px on desktop (what-we-do 32, index 34.6, build 41, single treatments 44, about 52.8, programmes 54.4, who comes 73.6, gifts 160). No two pages agree. On phone they run 26.9 to 64.
2. Index and what-we-do have a page title smaller than the headings below them. Index h1 is 34.6px against 54.4px section headings (30.4 vs 32 on phone). What-we-do h1 is 32px, in Karla, while its section headings are Quentin script at 44.9 to 115.2px. Who comes into your home and programmes also flip on phone (28.9 vs 32, 26.9 vs 35.2).
3. What-we-do is the only page whose h1 is in Karla. Every other page title is Instrument Serif (gifts is Quentin).
4. Section headings on the same page disagree. About uses 22 and 32. Index uses 25.6, 40, 46.1 and 54.4. What-we-do uses 22.4, 24, 30.2, 33.1, 54.7, 109.4, 115.2. Who comes into your home is the most consistent at 51.2 desktop.
5. Section headings switch between Instrument Serif and Quentin script across pages (programmes closing heading 48px Quentin, what-we-do headings Quentin, index headings serif). The Quentin script line itself is 38.1, 38.4, 55.7 and 79.2px on index alone, and 22.4 to 47.2px on phone.
6. FAQs has no h1 at all; its title is an h2 at 49px (18.7px question headings).
7. Body text is spread over about 25 distinct sizes, from 10.6 to 22px for real paragraphs (10.6, 10.9, 11, 12, 12.8, 13, 13.6, 13.7, 14, 15.1, 15.2, 15.7, 15.8, 16, 16.5, 16.6, 16.8, 17.3, 17.6, 18.4, 20, 20.4, 22). 16px is the most common but about (17.6) and what-we-do (16.6 to 20.4) differ, and the booking pages sit at 11 to 14.
8. Build your programme: choice names use 15.2px for card names but 44.8px for step headings, with sub lines at 13 and 15.2 mixed. Small print is 13, 14, 14.4 and 16 across pages.
9. Buttons and labels range from 11.5 to 13px with several off values (11.5, 12.8, 13, 13.1).

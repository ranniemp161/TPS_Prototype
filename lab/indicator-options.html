Three ways to show the light changing

Scroll each one. They use the real photographs, the real type and the real palette, so what you see is what the page would look like. Nothing here is wired into the live page.

Each behaves the way the real section does: the frame holds still and your scrolling drives the light from afternoon into night.

Option one

The words

Morning, afternoon, evening, night. The one you are in sits at full strength and the others stay faint, and the lit one travels as you scroll. The ink flips from dark to white partway down, the same way the menu chips do, so it stays readable as the room darkens.

Option two

The answer

No indicator at all. The question stays on screen, and once the light has gone the answer arrives with the booking button under it. The pull is wanting the answer rather than being told to scroll.

The horizon

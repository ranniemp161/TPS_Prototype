Who Comes Into Your Home

You are trusting someone to live in your home during one of the most vulnerable periods of your life. This page sets out who that person is, and the standard she is held to.

Meet Your Specialist

Your specialist is trained in both postpartum care and beauty therapy, so one person provides the care a maternity nurse and a separate therapist would otherwise divide between them.

Note for Amidat

A real name, background, photograph and years of experience are needed for this section before it can go live. Whether you are currently the sole specialist, or whether there is a wider team, should also be confirmed, since it changes how this page is written.

You Meet Her First

You are introduced to your specialist on your consultation call, before you commit to a booking. Nothing about who comes into your home is left until the day she arrives.

Vetting and Safety

Every specialist holds an enhanced DBS check, is trained in paediatric first aid, and has references that are checked before she joins the team. The Postpartum Suite carries full public liability and professional indemnity insurance.

Note for Amidat

Each of these must be confirmed as currently true before this page is published. Any line that does not apply should be removed rather than left as written.

If It Is Not the Right Match

If your specialist is not the right fit for your household, this can be discussed and addressed with us directly.

Note for Amidat

Your actual policy for changing a specialist needs to be confirmed, so this can be stated plainly rather than left general.

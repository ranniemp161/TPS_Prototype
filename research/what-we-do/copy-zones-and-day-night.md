## DAY AND NIGHT CARE

Care arranged around the hours you need it most.

For some mothers, the days feel full and the nights are manageable. For others, it is the 3am feed that tests them most. You choose when your specialist is with you, and the care she brings is the same either way.

### Day Care
7am to 7pm

Twelve hours of care from the moment your home wakes until the evening. Your day begins with a warm herbal bath and a nourishing breakfast, and continues with time to rest while your baby is cared for, your daily treatment, freshly cooked lunch and dinner, and your laundry and home taken care of. By 7pm the kitchen is spotless and you are ready to settle into the evening with your baby.

Ideal if you would like full support through the day and prefer to spend the nights with your baby yourselves.

### Day and Night Care
12pm to 6pm, then 12am to 6am

Six hours of care through the afternoon and six hours through the night, so you are supported when rest matters most.

Afternoon, 12pm to 6pm
Your specialist arrives in time to prepare a freshly cooked lunch, along with a lactation support drink or fresh tea. In the afternoon you enjoy your treatment, whether that is a restorative postpartum massage with hot stone therapy followed by your belly binding, or another treatment from your programme. While you rest, your baby is cared for and your laundry and home are taken care of. Before she finishes at 6pm, dinner is cooked and ready for you and your family, and the kitchen is left spotless.

Through the night, 12am to 6am
As the house goes quiet, your specialist takes over your baby's care. She settles your baby to sleep, wakes for every feed, and handles the nappy changes, winding and resettling in between. If you are breastfeeding, she brings your baby to you for each feed and settles them again afterwards, so you can return to sleep straight away. If you are bottle feeding, she feeds your baby herself so you can rest through the night. At 6am she hands your baby back to you with a short note on the night's feeds and sleep.

Ideal if the nights feel like the hardest part, and you would like unbroken rest alongside your treatments and meals.

## HERO (What We Do), received earlier the same day

After birth, who looks after mum?
We do.

The weeks that follow birth are some of the most important of a mother's life. Your body is healing from pregnancy and birth, your energy is being rebuilt, and the care you receive now can shape how you feel for months to come. Yet this is often the time when a mother's own recovery comes last. At The Postpartum Suite, we put it first. From your very first days at home with your baby, we care for you in four ways: support with you and your baby, restorative postpartum treatments, fresh nourishing meals and help around your home. Together, they give you what every new mother deserves: the time and space to truly heal.

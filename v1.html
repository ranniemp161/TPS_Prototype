What is Confinement?

In Malaysian and Southeast Asian traditions, the first month after birth, known as the confinement period, is a sacred window dedicated entirely to maternal rest, healing, and bonding.

Rather than expecting a mother to navigate the postpartum transition alone, confinement surrounds her with skilled daily support, nourishing food, and restorative bodywork.

At The Postpartum Suite, we bring this time-honoured practice into your home through dedicated live-in care from morning to evening.

Four kinds of care,
every single day.

Mother and baby

Hands-on support for your baby throughout the day so you can rest completely. Your dedicated specialist takes care of your newborn while you nap, recover, and bond, and is on hand to accompany you and your baby on outings.

Treatments

Hands-on therapeutic care integrated directly into your stay, not offered as a one-off treat. Every programme includes daily traditional belly binding, daily herbal baths, thermal blanket treatments, and scheduled postpartum massages with hot stone therapy, foot reflexology, and herbal foot spas.

Meals

Three fresh, tailored postpartum meals cooked daily in your own kitchen for you and your family. Your nutrition is supported throughout the day with traditional herbal teas and lactation drinks designed to restore your energy and assist feeding.

Home

Day-to-day household demands handled from the moment we arrive. Your specialist takes care of light housekeeping, organizing the baby’s wardrobe, grocery collection, and daily laundry and ironing.

How the site is travelled

Nobody reads this in the order of the menu.

This is a decision made over weeks, not minutes, and it is made by more than one person. Each page is written for the visit it will actually be read on. A page written for the first visit and read on the fourth feels thin. A page written for the fourth and met on the first is exhausting.

Visit one

What is this?

She has never heard of confinement care. She is feeling something rather than evaluating it.

Is this a real thing, and is it for someone like me?

Visit two

What actually happens?

Now she wants the detail. The day, the food, the treatments, the person.

What would this be like in my house?

Visit three

Can I trust them?

Scrutiny. Who is this woman, who checked her, what happens to my privacy, what about my situation.

What could go wrong, and what happens if it does?

Visit four

Let us do it.

Shape, fee, dates. Often with a partner who has seen none of the above.

What exactly are we buying, and how do we start?

Where the arguments land

What is communicated, and where.

Five arguments carry the whole site. They are not the same argument and they should not share a device. The governing rule beneath all of them: be generous with knowledge, silent about cost structure.

Magnitude delivered

The counterweight to a large fee is never a small unit price. It is a large delivery, itemised until the volume becomes physical. Three hundred and thirty hours. Ninety meals. Thirty baths.
Home, Programmes, the builder's summary panel

The unpriced line

Three or four countable lines with figures, then one line with no figure at all and no label. The absence is the device.
Home, and the close of every major page

The one window

A holiday can be rebooked. You get one first month with this baby, and it is happening now whether or not it is protected. The only genuine scarcity for a buyer who has money.
Home hero, About, What confinement is

One person, not four

To replicate the scope a household hires four people. For this clientele the argument is not the combined cost, it is the coordination: one relationship instead of four rotas, in the month she is least able to run them.
The Care, What confinement is

Discretion

Never asked out loud, and decisive. Who she is, who checked her, what is never discussed outside your home, and what happens if it is not working.

The shape of your stay

02 Length of programme

Thirty days is the full confinement period, kept the way tradition intends. Changing the length resets the massage rhythm to the standard for that length, so you are always starting from the published programme.

01 Your care format

Live-in means your postpartum specialist stays in your home for the whole programme. Daytime in-home care means she arrives each morning and leaves in the evening, and carries a supplement of £30 a day towards her accommodation and travel.

03 Daytime, or day and night

Daytime care runs 7am to 7pm. With night care your specialist starts at 1pm, settles at 7pm, takes your baby from 11.30pm and hands back at 6am, so you sleep through. Night care is available on live-in programmes.

Through the day

04 Meals and nourishment

Cooked fresh in your kitchen, for you and your family. Herbal teas and lactation drinks are part of every programme.

05 Herbal baths

Drawn with herbs chosen for the stage of your recovery, and run for you rather than left for you to run. Five baths across this programme.

Your own recovery

06 Recovery treatments

The figure beside each one is how many times it runs across the programme you have chosen. Those counts are set by the length, not by you.

07 Massage rhythm

Our 5-day programme is built around four times a week. Choose more or less and the fee moves with it. Massage is never taken on consecutive days, so your body has a day between treatments.
